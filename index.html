About Me

Hey there! My name is Busari Habiblahi Adeleke, but you can call me Habib. I'm a technology enthusiast with a genuine interest in building things and exploring what technology can do. I have an academic background in Microbiology and Science Laboratory Technology, holding an HND and OND, respectively.

I've always been drawn to technology, and over the years, I've developed an interest in website development. Now, I'm taking things a step further at AltSchool Africa's School of Engineering, where I'm pursuing a Diploma in Software Engineering through the AI-Powered Fullstack Engineering programme.

I'm currently learning how to bring ideas to life through code, understand how different technologies work together, and build applications that solve real-world problems. I enjoy researching, experimenting, trying new things, and learning from the process. I'm excited about this journey, the challenges ahead, and hopefully, the amazing things I'll get to build along the way. Who knows? This might just be the beginning of something great!

Why I Joined the Program

I joined the School of Engineering program to develop practical software engineering skills and understand how websites and applications are designed, built, tested, and deployed.

I also want a structured learning environment where I can practise consistently, work on projects, receive feedback, and learn alongside other people developing their technology skills.

My Goals for the School of Engineering Program

  1. Build a strong foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and web development.
  2. Understand both frontend and backend development.
  3. Learn how APIs, databases, servers, and web applications work together.
  4. Build real-world projects that demonstrate my skills.
  5. Improve my problem-solving and software engineering abilities.
  6. Use AI tools responsibly to improve my development workflow.
  7. Finish the program with a portfolio that reflects my growth.
